Traditional Arts

This is a famous painting that every Japanese person knows: The Great Wave off Kanagawa (Fugaku Sanjurokkei Kanagawa-oki Namiura) is one of the most iconic works of Japanese art, instantly recognizable to nearly everyone in Japan. Created by the renowned ukiyo-e artist Katsushika Hokusai, this masterpiece is part of his celebrated series Thirty-six Views of Mount Fuji (Fugaku Sanjurokkei), produced during the late Edo period.
