Destination

  • Nature
    • Mount Fuji (Yamanashi Prefecture/Shizuoka Prefecture) - Japan's iconic and beautiful mountain. Perfect for hiking and enjoying nearby hot springs.
    • Takachiho Gorge (Miyazaki Prefecture) - A mystical gorge ideal for boating and walking.
    • Shirakawa-go (Gifu Prefecture) - A UNESCO World Heritage site with traditional thatched-roof houses. Especially stunning in winter.
    • Okinawa Islands (Ishigaki Island, Miyako Island, etc.) - Paradise with crystal-clear waters and white sandy beaches.
  • History and Culture
    • Kyoto (Kyoto Prefecture) - A city full of Japanese tradition and beauty, with attractions like Kinkaku-ji, Kiyomizu-dera, and Fushimi Inari Taisha.
    • Nara (Nara Prefecture) - Home to Todai-ji Temple, Kasuga Taisha Shrine, and the friendly deer of Nara Park.
    • Hiroshima Peace Memorial Park (Hiroshima Prefecture) - Learn history at the Peace Memorial Museum and Atomic Bomb Dome.
    • Himeji Castle (Hyogo Prefecture) - Known as the "White Heron Castle," it is one of Japan's most beautiful castles.
  • Cities and Entertainment
    • Tokyo (Tokyo Prefecture) - A vibrant city with attractions like Senso-ji Temple, Tokyo Skytree, Shibuya Crossing, and Odaiba.
    • Osaka (Osaka Prefecture) - Known for Universal Studios Japan (USJ), Dotonbori, and Tsutenkaku Tower.
    • Yokohama (Kanagawa Prefecture) - Enjoy shopping and dining at Minato Mirai, Chinatown, and Yokohama Landmark Tower.
